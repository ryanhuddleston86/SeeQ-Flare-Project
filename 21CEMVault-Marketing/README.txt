BRAND BASICS
------------
Colors:  navy #0F2438 · slate #1C3A56 · amber #E08E2B · steel #EDEFF2 · gray #8CA0B3
Type:    Space Grotesk — CEM 700, Vault 500. Space Mono for technical labels.
Tagline: "Every reading, audit-ready."
Descriptor: "Continuous emissions monitoring & downtime compliance for refineries."

RULES
-----
• Amber is the single accent — one active cell / "Vault" / a thin rule. Never more.
• Keep one cell-width of clear space around the mark; use favicon below ~24px.
• Don't stretch, rotate, recolor, or add effects to the mark.
